Andrés Ruiz Palomino

Gameplay systems, editor tooling and the feel layer on top of them.

I build gameplay systems and the editor tools designers use to iterate on them: ability systems, state machines, cameras, and the feel layer that makes a hit land (hitstop, knockback, feedback). The rule I work by is that adding content should mean adding data, not writing code, so most of what I build ends up data-driven and authored in the editor.

I also design the systems I build. I run a Spanish-language YouTube channel about game design, so I show up to a feature with an opinion on why it should feel a certain way and I can argue the trade-off with a designer instead of just implementing the ticket. That overlap, systems programmer with a technical designer's eye, is the useful part of my profile.

Three years across small indie teams, wearing systems, tooling, tech art and production hats. Currently a programmer on a live-service Unity 6 mobile title, and building a 3D action combat prototype solo on nights and weekends.
